Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how video projects work.

What formats do you deliver?

Vertical (9:16) or widescreen (16:9) video, in 1080p or 4K, as MP4 or MOV files.

When does work begin?

Once the first 50% payment and all project materials have been received. The remaining 50% is due after you approve the final version, before the final files are delivered.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds at no extra charge. Each round is one combined set of notes covering changes to shots, text, effects and timing. A new concept, restarting production or extra deliverables are quoted separately.

Will I receive the project files?

You receive the final exported videos. Editable project files (Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve) aren't included, but can be provided for an additional fee.

How can I use the finished video?

Once the project is paid in full, you can use and publish the final video for the agreed purposes. Music, fonts and stock footage remain under their own licenses.

Will my project appear in your portfolio?

Only after you've released it publicly, and not at all if we agree on confidentiality beforehand. Unreleased material is never shared without your permission.

What if I need to cancel?

There's no fixed cancellation fee. If you cancel before work starts, your first payment is refunded, minus any approved costs that can't be recovered. After work starts, the settlement reflects the work completed.
